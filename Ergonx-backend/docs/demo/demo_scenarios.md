   roles, HR, payroll, and accounting access.
2. Sign in as HR Admin to review the pending annual leave request for
   `EMP-000109` and the employee/onboarding experience.

   interviews, offers, and the linked hired candidate scenario.
4. Open Attendance to show fixed, flexible, and night schedules; approved
   overtime for `EMP-000113`; and pending overtime for `EMP-000114`.
5. Open Payroll to show the finalized September 2026 run and payslips.
6. Open Accounting to show the posted payroll journal, vendor bill/payment,
