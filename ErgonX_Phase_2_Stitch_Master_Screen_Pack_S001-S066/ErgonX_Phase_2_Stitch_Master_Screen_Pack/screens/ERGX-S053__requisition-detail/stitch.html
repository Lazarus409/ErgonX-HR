Role description

A summary of the role, core pedagogical commitments, and institutional charter.

The School of Computing at Rivermere University invites applications for a permanent full-time Lecturer in Computer Science. The successful candidate will contribute to high-quality teaching, research, and research-informed pedagogical practice across our undergraduate and postgraduate degree programmes.

Primary responsibilities will include syllabus development in algorithmic design, cloud infrastructure, and modern software paradigms. The appointed academic will establish an active, peer-reviewed research agenda supported by competitive external funding applications, while actively participating in institutional assessment, pastoral student support, and academic governance.

Key requirements

Mandatory and preferred qualifications required for academic appointment.

check_circle Essential Criteria
  • PhD in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or closely related discipline.
  • Demonstrated excellence in teaching at undergraduate and/or postgraduate level.
  • Active research track record with recognized high-impact publications (REF 3* / 4*).
  • Strong communication, interpersonal skills, and collegiate leadership acumen.
  • Uncompromising commitment to inclusive education and equitable student mentorship.
stars Desirable Criteria
  • Proven track record in securing competitive external research grants (e.g. EPSRC/UKRI).
  • Experience supervising doctoral and master's postgraduate research students.
  • Demonstrated experience in collaborative cross-faculty curriculum modernization.
  • Industry partnerships and professional chartered accreditation (BCS / IEEE Fellow).
  • Familiarity with hybrid and distance digital learning environments.
