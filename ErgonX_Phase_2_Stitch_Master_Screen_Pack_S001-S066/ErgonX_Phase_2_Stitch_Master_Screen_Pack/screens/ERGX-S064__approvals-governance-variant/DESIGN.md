## Brand & Style

The design system establishes an institutional-grade, highly structured visual environment designed for complex enterprise resource planning, corporate governance, multi-entity accounting, and statutory oversight. It prioritizes clarity over ornamentation, structural density over loose decorative padding, and rapid executive scanning over casual consumption.

### Target Audience
C-suite finance executives, multi-entity controllers, enterprise auditors, operational directors, and institutional administrators requiring real-time, error-free oversight and mass-transaction processing.

### Emotional Response
- **Uncompromised Authority:** Grounded deep midnight foundations anchor the interface, conveying regulatory security, compliance, and institutional stability.
- **Architectural Clarity:** Strict line weights, neutral container elevations, and disciplined alignment eliminate ambiguity across data grids and complex financial workflows.
- **Controlled Precision:** Kinetic vibrant accents direct visual focus purely toward critical actions, pending approvals, and systemic deviations.

### Design Movement
**Corporate Modernist Precision:** A rigorous intersection of contemporary Scandinavian utilitarianism and Swiss structural data layout. Unified typography paired with razor-sharp division lines, crisp white surfaces resting over slate-tinted canvases, and restrained, intentional kinetic accents across primary milestones.

## Colors

The palette is engineered for cognitive endurance in data-dense financial and statutory environments. High-contrast midnight anchors define structural navigation and command shells, balanced against pure light workspaces and strict semantic governance indicators.

### Structural Anchors
- **Primary Foundation Shell (`#0E1C2F` / `#000000`):** The structural midnight bedrock applied to persistent left rail navigation, executive modal headers, and primary data contrast anchors.
- **Interactive Cobalt Accent (`#0051D5`):** Reserved strictly for high-priority interactive states, active conversion triggers, focused boundaries, and operational confirmations.
- **Deep Cyan Anchor (`#001F26` / `#0090A9`):** Tertiary indicator for structural sub-groupings and audit trails.

### Work Surfaces & Borders
- **Application Canvas (`#F8F9FF`):** A cool-tinted off-white background mitigating eye fatigue during prolonged analytical sessions.
- **Card Surfaces (`#FFFFFF`):** High-opacity pure white panels creating distinct foreground separation.
- **Dividers & Structural Borders (`#C5C6CD` / `#75777D`):** Consistent 1px neutral barriers separating ledger data cells, toolbar actions, and grouped modules.

### Semantic Status Spectrum
- **Success (`#059669` / `#ECFDF5`):** Approved, posted, reconciled, balanced journals.
- **Pending / Warning (`#D97706` / `#FFFBEB`):** Approval pending, variance detected, awaiting secondary signature.
- **Critical / Danger (`#BA1A1A` / `#FFDAD6`):** Overdue, validation failure, segregation-of-duties conflict, voided.
- **In-Progress / Informational (`#0051D5` / `#EFF4FF`):** Processing batch, draft state, open period.

## Typography

The typography is unified across all architectural layers, display headers, tabular controls, and dense data views using **DM Sans**. This geometric, understated sans-serif provides exceptional rhythm, clean clarity, and consistent optical weight across both high-level executive dashboards and detailed financial ledger grids.

### Tabular Numerals & Ledger Data
All numerical fields—including ledger amounts, transaction identifiers, timestamps, and balance figures—must apply `font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1, "cv05" 1`. This prevents column drift and guarantees strict vertical decimal alignment across data-dense rows.

### Type Roles
- **Display & Headlines (`DM Sans`):** Bold and semibold weights (`700`, `600`) establish structural authority across module headers, executive KPIs, and primary view titles.
- **Body & Data Grid Content (`DM Sans`):** Regular weight (`400`) at 12px to 15px sizes balances dense information packing with effortless legibility.
- **Labels & Micro-Tags (`DM Sans`):** Semibold and bold weights (`600`, `700`) paired with subtle positive tracking preserve legibility in dense status badges and table column caps.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is engineered around an 8pt metric rhythm with 4pt micro-steps for compact data components, enforcing clear functional boundaries across institutional workspaces.

### Global Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (>= 1280px):** Fixed-width navigation sidebar (`260px` expanded, `72px` icon rail collapsed) docked to a dynamic fluid content viewport. The content zone utilizes a 12-column grid with `1.5rem` (24px) gutters and `1.5rem` outer canvas padding.
- **Tablet / Split Screen (768px - 1279px):** Sidebar converts into an overlay drawer. The workspace transitions to an 8-column layout with `1rem` gutters. Slide-out inspection panels overlay up to 420px of screen real estate.
- **Mobile PWA Shell (< 768px):** Single-column stack with `1rem` canvas margins, a persistent 56px top app bar housing contextual institution controls, and a 64px bottom navigation bar pinned to the viewport base.

### Component Spacing Principles
- Metric cards maintain `space-lg` internal padding with `space-md` gaps between adjacent tiles.
- Financial tables enforce compact vertical density: `0.625rem` (10px) vertical cell padding for dense transaction sheets and `1rem` (16px) for standard records.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses crisp architectural surfaces, 1px perimeter definition, and low-diffused structural drop shadows. Pure flat styling is avoided to keep floating panels and bulk flyouts visually distinct from foundational canvases.

### Surface Hierarchy Levels
- **Base Canvas (Level 0):** `#F8F9FF` slate-tinted canvas. No shadow.
- **Card & Data Panels (Level 1):** `#FFFFFF` surfaces with a 1px solid border (`#C5C6CD`) and an ambient, low-opacity drop shadow:
  `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(11, 28, 48, 0.05), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(11, 28, 48, 0.05);`
- **Flyout Drawers, Context Menus & Popovers (Level 2):** Elevated interactive components with strict edge borders and heightened atmospheric dispersion:
  `box-shadow: 0 10px 15px -3px rgba(11, 28, 48, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(11, 28, 48, 0.04);`
- **Executive Modals & Critical Overlays (Level 3):** Centered floating modals supported by a 40% `#0B1C30` backdrop overlay blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(4px)`):
  `box-shadow: 0 20px 25px -5px rgba(11, 28, 48, 0.12), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(11, 28, 48, 0.06);`

### Border Philosophy
Shadows never act alone. Every elevated surface must be bounded by a 1px `#C5C6CD` or `#75777D` border to preserve razor-sharp distinction under low-contrast enterprise displays.

## Shapes

The design system employs a soft, highly restrained corner radius scale (`roundedness: 1`). Rounding is intentionally kept minimal to maintain an executive, institutional appearance and avoid informal, consumer-oriented curves.

### Corner Token Application
- **Input Fields, Dropdowns & Standard Buttons:** `rounded` (`0.25rem` / 4px). Delivers crisp architectural boundaries that align cleanly with dense financial tables.
- **Metric Tiles, Data Cards & Modals:** `rounded-lg` (`0.5rem` / 8px). Softens large perimeter containers without sacrificing data density.
- **Status Badges, System Pills & Avatars:** Fully rounded (`rounded-full` / 9999px) to create clear differentiation between actionable square inputs and passive categorical tags.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary:** Background `#0051D5` (Cobalt) or `#000000` (Structural Midnight). Text `#FFFFFF`, font family `DM Sans`, weight 600, border-radius `4px`, padding `8px 16px`. Active press effect reduces opacity to 90% without vertical shifts.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Background `#FFFFFF`, 1px border `#C5C6CD`, text `#0B1C30`. Hover shifts border to `#75777D` and background to `#EFF4FF`.
- **Destructive:** Border and text `#BA1A1A`, hover background `#FFDAD6`.

### Enterprise Status Badges & Audit Pills
- **Geometry:** Height 22px, `rounded-full` (9999px), horizontal padding `8px`, typography `label-sm` (`DM Sans`).
- **Visuals:** Light-tint container with saturated label text and an optional 6px circular dot indicator:
  - *Success (Approved/Posted):* `#ECFDF5` container, `#059669` text and dot.
  - *Pending (Under Review):* `#FFFBEB` container, `#D97706` text and dot.
  - *Danger (Overdue/Rejected):* `#FFDAD6` container, `#BA1A1A` text and dot.
  - *Draft / Neutral:* `#EFF4FF` container, `#44474C` text and dot.

### Data Tables & Grid View
- **Header:** Background `#EFF4FF`, uppercase `label-md` (`DM Sans`) tracking, 1px bottom border `#C5C6CD`, column sorting chevron indicator with 4px gap.
- **Row States:** Default `#FFFFFF`, hover `#F8F9FF` transition (150ms ease). Selected row adopts `#E5EEFF` background with `#0051D5` 2px left border indicator.
- **Tabular Data:** Right-aligned numerical values using `DM Sans` with tabular figures enabled; left-aligned contextual metadata.

### Form Inputs & High-Contrast Controls
- **Text & Select Inputs:** Height 36px, border 1px solid `#C5C6CD`, background `#FFFFFF`, border-radius 4px, font family `DM Sans`. Focus state: `#0051D5` border with `0 0 0 2px rgba(0, 81, 213, 0.15)`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** 16px square/circle, border 1.5px solid `#75777D`. Selected state renders `#0051D5` fill with centered white checkmark icon.

### Cards & Container Panels
- Bound by a 1px `#C5C6CD` perimeter border and resting on a `#FFFFFF` surface with Level 1 ambient elevation. Header regions feature `title-md` or `headline-sm` in `DM Sans` paired with contextual utility controls.

### Drawer & Inspector Panels
- Fixed to the right viewport edge with a `420px` default width (expandable to `560px` for multi-stage workflows). Contains a sticky header in `DM Sans`, close trigger, scrollable internal content zone, and a fixed bottom utility bar for multi-item batch execution.
